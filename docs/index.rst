Artificial Benchmark for Community Detection
============================================

Artificial Benchmark for Community Detection. ABCD is a method for
sampling random graphs with known community structure, and is useful
for benchmarking community detection algorithms. The generated graphs
can be have similar properties to those generated by LFR, but ABCD is
much faster and has a noise parameter `xi` in [0,1] that transitions
from disjoint communities to no communities.
